About Dave Graham

Professional live entertainment. Full-band sound. One performer.

One Performer. Full-Band Sound.

I'm Dave Graham, a solo entertainer delivering the sound and energy of a full band without the expense or complexity of hiring one. With vocals, guitar, keyboards, and professionally produced backing tracks, I bring a full, polished musical experience to private parties, corporate events, weddings, and live music venues.

My approach is simple: play music people know and love, keep the energy moving, and make the entertainment part of your event easy. From rock, pop, and country to oldies and crowd favorites, I tailor the show to the room and take requests when the moment's right.

More Than Just Music

Outside of music, I'm a martial arts instructor with more than 20 years of experience. I'm also a mail carrier by day, so professionalism, reliability, and showing up ready to work aren't just part of the show—they're part of who I am.

Whether I'm teaching on the mat or performing on stage, I believe in preparation, commitment, and giving people an experience worth remembering.

Planning a private celebration, corporate event, wedding, or looking for live entertainment at your venue? Let's talk about making it a great night.
